Jesus is the Vine - followers are branches


Here is JESUS speaking:

John 15:1-2  Amplified Bible:

> **I am the true Vine, and My Father is the vinedresser. Every branch in Me that does not bear fruit, He takes away; and every branch that continues to bear fruit, He [repeatedly] prunes, so that it will bear more fruit [even richer and finer fruit].**

We have to be in JESUS, placed our faith in Him, been baptised in water for repentance, and received the HOLY SPIRIT in us.

John 15:4-7  Amplified Bible:

> **Remain in Me, and I [will remain] in you. Just as no branch can bear fruit by itself without remaining in the vine, neither can you [bear fruit, producing evidence of your faith] unless you remain in Me. I am the Vine; you are the branches. The one who remains in Me and I in him bears much fruit, for [otherwise] apart from Me [that is, cut off from vital union with Me] you can do nothing. If anyone does not remain in Me, he is thrown out like a [broken off] branch, and withers and dies; and they gather such branches and throw them into the fire, and they are burned. If you remain in Me and My words remain in you [that is, if we are vitally united and My message lives in your heart], ask whatever you wish and it will be done for you.**

We must go about our lives in constant relationship and union with GOD. We can't go off doing our own thing and living like the world lives. We must learn to listen to GOD each and every day. Learn to hear their voice. 

John 15:8-10  Amplified Bible:

> **My Father is glorified and honored by this, when you bear much fruit, and prove yourselves to be My [true] disciples. I have loved you just as the Father has loved Me; remain in My love [and do not doubt My love for you]. If you keep My commandments and obey My teaching, you will remain in My love, just as I have kept My Father’s commandments and remain in His love.**

This part is important because we can't just go off and live a life of sin thinking that we were once saved we will always be saved. No, it says ***keep*** His commandments and ***obey*** His teaching. Otherwise we won't remain in His love and we'll be thrown out like a broken off branch, withering and dying.

What are some of those commandments? Read your Bible to figure it out. But here's two in the next few verses:

John 15:12-14, 17  Amplified Bible

> **“This is My commandment, that you love and unselfishly seek the best for one another, just as I have loved you. No one has greater love [nor stronger commitment] than to lay down his own life for his friends. You are my friends if you keep on doing what I command you.**

> **This [is what] I command you: that you love and unselfishly seek the best for one another.**

This basic statement ***love and unselfishly seek the best for one another*** is like a blanket rule covering a whole heap of behaviour ruling out a whole heap of "do nots" like the obvious stealing, killing, destroying and only caring about yourself. Love each other, don't be selfish. It's that simple. And we have the HOLY SPIRIT to help us.
