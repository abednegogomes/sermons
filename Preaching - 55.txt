In their greed they will exploit you


In [Deuteronomy 15:1-18](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deuteronomy%2015&version=AMP) it states that every seven years, the Israelites are commanded to completely forgive the debts of their fellow countrymen and generously support the poor without resentment, so there will be no poor among them, ensuring a blessed and self-sufficient nation.

I wonder, as Christians, who are thought as the spiritual expansion of Israel under the new covenant as per [Ephesians 2:12-19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians%202%3A12-19&version=AMP), why we don't also follow this generous doctrine of debt relief for fellow Christians and making sure there is no poor among us?

It seems heartbreaking that mega churches spend so much money on building maintenance, decoration and upkeep, also paying for pastors to have private jets. This seems to follow a twisting of tithing rules in [Deuteronomy 14:22-29](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=deuteronomy%2014:22-29&version=AMP) to claim that tithes and donations are owed to their church organisation, but the organisation generally neglects the poor and needy. Meanwhile however, the people still are required to tithe 10% of their income to these organisations. I guarantee a low cost church service could be held in a basic mega-tent in a field somewhere, which doesn't require fancy multi-million dollar buildings or strobe lighting to make a fancy performance. The focus should be on GOD and the community of believers being built up into a *spiritual* house [1 Peter 2:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20peter%202%3A5-9&version=AMP):

> **You [believers], like living stones, are being built up into a spiritual house for a holy and dedicated priesthood, to offer spiritual sacrifices [that are] acceptable and pleasing to God through Jesus Christ.**

What are acceptable spiritual sacrifices?

Hebrews 13:15-16:

> **Through Him, therefore, let us at all times offer up to God a sacrifice of praise, which is the fruit of lips that thankfully acknowledge and confess and glorify His name. Do not neglect to do good, to contribute [to the needy of the church as an expression of fellowship], for such sacrifices are always pleasing to God.**

Psalm 51:17 Amplified Bible:

> **My [only] sacrifice [acceptable] to God is a broken spirit; A broken and contrite heart [broken with sorrow for sin, thoroughly penitent], such, O God, You will not despise.**

Romans 12:1 Amplified Bible:

> **Therefore I urge you, brothers and sisters, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies [dedicating all of yourselves, set apart] as a living sacrifice, holy and well-pleasing to God, which is your rational (logical, intelligent) act of worship.** 

Psalm 141:2 Amplified Bible:

> **Let my prayer be counted as incense before You; The lifting up of my hands as the evening offering.**

NB: There is no mention of sacrificing money to church organisations who are largely spending the donated money on itself, its operations and its staff. So heed the warning in 2 Peter 2:1-3 Amplified Bible:

> **But [in those days] false prophets arose among the people, just as there will be false teachers among you, who will subtly introduce destructive heresies, even denying the Master who bought them, bringing swift destruction on themselves. Many will follow their shameful ways, and because of them the way of truth will be maligned. *And in their greed they will exploit you* with false arguments and twisted doctrine. Their sentence [of condemnation which God has decreed] from a time long ago is not idle [but is still in force], and their destruction and deepening misery is not asleep [but is on its way].**

James 1:27 Amplified Bible:

> **Pure and unblemished religion [as it is expressed in outward acts] in the sight of our God and Father is this: to visit and look after the fatherless and the widows in their distress, and to keep oneself uncontaminated by the [secular] world.**

Mark 12:30-31 Amplified Bible:

> **AND YOU SHALL LOVE THE LORD YOUR GOD WITH ALL YOUR HEART, AND WITH ALL YOUR SOUL (life), AND WITH ALL YOUR MIND (thought, understanding), AND WITH ALL YOUR STRENGTH.’ This is the second: ‘You shall [unselfishly] LOVE YOUR NEIGHBOR AS YOURSELF.’ There is no other commandment greater than these.”**

Don't love your local mega church and their avaricious demands for you to tithe to them so they can afford their luxury lifestyles. Go help the poor and needy instead.
