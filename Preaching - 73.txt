The golden rule


[Judges 1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Judges%201&version=AMP):7 Amplified Bible:

> Adoni-bezek said, “Seventy kings with their thumbs and big toes cut off used to gather up scraps of food under my table; **as I have done [to others], so God has repaid me**.” So they brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.

This is an important lesson of the Bible, if you do evil to others (and are unrepentant to GOD for it and won't make amends), GOD will repay you the same way. This is like a spiritual law.

Hosea 8:7 Amplified Bible:

> **For they sow the wind [in evil] And they reap the whirlwind [in disaster].** The standing grain has no growth; It yields no grain. If it were to yield, strangers would swallow it up.

Matthew 26:52 Amplified Bible:

> Then Jesus said to him, “**Put your sword back in its place; for all those who *habitually* draw the sword will die by the sword**.

So what have we learned in how we should live? This verse sums it up well, in Matthew 7:12 Amplified Bible:

> “So then, **in everything treat others the same way you want them to treat you**, for this is [the essence of] the Law and the [writings of the] Prophets.
