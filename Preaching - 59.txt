Defeating the anakim


Joshua 14:11-12 Amplified Bible:

> **I am still as strong today as I was the day Moses sent me; as my strength was then, so is my strength now, for war and for going out and coming in. So now, give me this hill country about which the Lord spoke that day, for you heard on that day that the [giant-like] Anakim were there, with great fortified cities; perhaps the Lord will be with me, and I shall drive them out just as the Lord said.”**

I thought this was interesting, these giant like anakim, had to be defeated to conquer the land, they must have been ancestors of goliath, as we hear of later in [1 Samuel 17](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20Samuel%2017&version=AMP). In this battle, in the next chapter in [Joshua 15](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Joshua%2015%3A13-17&version=AMP) one of Caleb's nephews, Othniel, captures the city, probably with many fighting men helping. I got Google Gemini to generate an example of that battle [here](https://i.imgur.com/UEnVr7H.jpeg) if that helps describe it visually. If goliath is anything to go by (height of six cubits and a span), they could be anywhere up to about nine feet, ten inches tall (3.00m).

Those early Israelites showed amazing courage to defeat such giants and they relied heavily on the LORD for victory. We should show courage and fight too, against whatever giants we are facing today with prayer *and* [worship](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=awkO61T6i0k), whether the giants are spiritual (e.g. unbelief), physical in our bodies (e.g. a health issue), work (e.g. some difficult problem), family (e.g. relationship or children issues), or praying about serious issues in our countries.
