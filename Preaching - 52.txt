Deliverance with oil

Mark 6:12-13 Amplified Bible:

> **So they went out and preached that men should repent [that is, think differently, recognize sin, turn away from it, and live changed lives]. And they were casting out many demons and were anointing with oil many who were sick, and healing them.**

I have watched a number of deliverance services e.g. [things like this](https://youtu.be/HxT8OLeSeyg?si=X4QmFWt4VXClogpR) and have seen such things in Brazil, but it amazes me how few use oil in deliverance.

Isaiah 10:27 21st Century King James Version:

> **And it shall come to pass in that day, that his burden shall be taken away from off thy shoulder and his yoke from off thy neck, and the yoke shall be destroyed because of the anointing.**

The oil signifies:

 - The Presence of the HOLY SPIRIT: Throughout the Old Testament, oil is the premier symbol of the HOLY SPIRIT's presence, illumination, and power. Just as oil seeps into and saturates whatever it touches, the HOLY SPIRIT fills and empowers a person.
 
 - Consecration (Setting Apart): In ancient Israel, priests, prophets, and kings were anointed with oil to show they were specifically chosen and set apart by GOD for a holy purpose. When the disciples anointed the sick, it was a symbolic way of saying: "GOD sees you, you are set apart for His mercy, and His kingdom has claimed you."
 
 - The Break of Bondage: Isaiah 10:27 famously states that "the yoke shall be destroyed because of the anointing." Using oil tied physical healing directly to spiritual liberation from the oppression of sickness and evil.
 
Anecdotally, in some pentecostal services in Brazil I went to, they would anoint people at the beginning of the service in a line, and on some of the people, just putting the oil on their head, was enough to get someone manifesting immediately. They would start flailing around and often fall over, requiring the pastors or helpers to have to restrain them and pray for them further before they would return to normal.

Whenever they did **not** use oil, the deliverance would take longer and there would be a prolonged power encounter with the evil spirit/s. Or the evil spirits would be able to stay hidden and go home again with the person continuing to cause chaos in their lives and families. And you can see that the deliverance actually takes a prolonged amount of time in some of this guy's videos in the YouTube video linked above.

Obviously going to a deliverance service like that could be embarrassing for people, especially if they're filming it. But I believe if you get down on your knees, truly repent of your sin, anoint yourself with some oil from your kitchen (e.g. olive oil), invite the HOLY SPIRIT to fill you, and speaking out loud command every evil spirit out of you, in the name of JESUS, this will be sufficient and they will leave. As with any addiction to substances, cigarettes, alcohol, pornography, sex etc, a complete food fast of 3 days and prayer is also usually sufficient and very effective to break these addictions completely.
