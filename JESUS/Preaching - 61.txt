No time to tarry


Jude 14-16 Amplified Bible:

> **It was about these people that Enoch, in the seventh generation from Adam, prophesied, when he said, “Look, the Lord came with myriads of His holy ones to execute judgment upon all, and to convict all the ungodly of all the ungodly deeds they have done in an ungodly way, and of all the harsh and cruel things ungodly sinners have spoken against Him.” These people are [habitual] murmurers, griping and complaining, following after their own desires [controlled by passion]; they speak arrogantly, [pretending admiration and] flattering people to gain an advantage.**

People [seem to think](https://www.reddit.com/r/Christianity/comments/1u6b66z/genocide_in_the_bible/) GOD should be light-handed on sinners and the ungodly. The repentant (that turn from their sin) and that accept JESUS as LORD and Saviour, sure, they will receive grace and mercy. But the unrepentant unbelievers will get what is coming to them.

We don't get to choose who GOD is, or what they believe is sin or not, or how they choose to judge humanity. GOD is GOD. You can argue to your heart's content about what is sin and live your life any way you want, but one day *there will be judgement*.

Hebrews 9:27 Amplified Bible:

> **And just as it is appointed and destined for all men to die once and after this [comes certain] judgment,**

Don't waste the day thinking there is going to be a tomorrow for you. Many die unexpectedly and from mysterious causes. Don't hope in a happy afterlife if you haven't accepted JESUS as your LORD and Saviour and are living in wilful sin. There is no time to tarry in making that decision. And there's no time to procrastinate in living in sinful, worldly ways hoping that you can just repent another day in the future.
