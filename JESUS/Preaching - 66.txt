Mark of the beast


Revelation 13:16-18 Amplified Bible:

> **Also he compels all, the small and the great, and the rich and the poor, and the free men and the slaves, to be given a mark on their right hand or on their forehead [signifying allegiance to the beast], and that no one will be able to buy or sell, except the one who has the mark, either the name of the beast or the number of his name. Here is wisdom. Let the person who has enough insight calculate the number of the beast, for it is the [imperfect] number of a man; and his number is six hundred and sixty-six.**


Revelation 14:9-11 Amplified Bible:

> **Doom for Worshipers of the Beast**

> **Then another angel, a third one, followed them, saying with a loud voice, “Whoever worships the beast and his image and receives the mark [of the beast] on his forehead or on his hand, he too will [have to] drink of the wine of the wrath of God, mixed undiluted into the cup of His anger; and he will be tormented with fire and brimstone (flaming sulfur) in the presence of the holy angels and in the presence of the Lamb (Christ). And the smoke of their torment ascends forever and ever; and they have no rest day and night—those who worship the beast and his image, and whoever receives the mark of his name.”**

I found [this site](https://www.antichristcalculator.com) which has calculations for various gematria of who the beast could be. You can input the *full* names of various famous people and see if it calculates to 666.

I sometimes wonder what the mark could be. In the past people thought it might be a bar code, or QR code. These days people are thinking it could be a computer chip with AI in it, and with a brain-computer interface like neuralink. Something in which the ultimate goal is to use symbiosis to transform humans so their brains are seamlessly connected into the global internet and AI systems to collaborate. [This video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67K7x7JuBPg) explains recent developments with an AI chip that turns your hand into a supercomputer.

This is really worrying from a faith point of view, because you would be so connected in with AI and the internet in your head you may lose what it means to be human. If a third-party company or government can access the data layer of your brain, your innermost thoughts could be monitored, commercialised, or hacked. If everyone is connected to the same AI cloud for answers and reasoning, human thought could become entirely homogenized, wiping out the cognitive diversity that drives creativity. Or you may lose your ability to have connection with GOD and become more like a borg in the collective, unable to be influenced by the HOLY SPIRIT any longer. And as humans we **need** the HOLY SPIRIT who will guide us, correct us and bring us along the path to righteousness.

Ephesians 1:13-14 Amplified Bible:

> **In Him, you also, when you heard the word of truth, the good news of your salvation, and [as a result] believed in Him, were stamped with the seal of the promised Holy Spirit [the One promised by Christ] as owned and protected [by God]. The Spirit is the guarantee [the first installment, the pledge, a foretaste] of our inheritance until the redemption of God’s own [purchased] possession [His believers], to the praise of His glory.**

I think we're better off throwing in our lot with GOD, rather than joining the AI collective.
