The rapture


Firstly, has GOD told me that the rapture will happen: **yes**. Have they told me when exactly? *No*. Have I had a dream about being taken up in the sky with others towards a white bright light: **yes**. Have others had dreams about the rapture? **yes**.

The time is nigh.

No matter what _you_ believe (pre-tribulation, post-tribulation, etc) you must seek personal relationship with GOD yourself and ask JESUS to be your LORD and Saviour. You can't borrow other people's faith, your pope's faith, your pastor's faith, and somehow hope to be taken with them. Your relationship/friendship/walk with GOD and each member of the Trinity is your own and individual. Don't be like [the foolish virgins](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%2025&version=AMP) who were waiting but didn't bring enough oil (their active faith and HOLY SPIRIT presence and fruit in their life was weak).

So, time to get serious about your walk with GOD. Did you know you can talk to each member of the Trinity yourself? Just start a prayer/conversation with them by name e.g. "Dear FATHER GOD, ...", or "Dear LORD JESUS...", or "Dear HOLY SPIRIT..." and ask your questions. Get into a quiet space and _listen_ for the answer. You can also write these down if that's easier and less confusing to hear.

Also, if you can't hear clearly, is there some lie you're believing about GOD? Ask whoever you feel most comfortable talking with if there's a lie you're believing about them. Repent and renounce the lie. Keep going until you hear clearly.

Have you given your life to JESUS and been water baptised? It's hard to hear from GOD if you don't have the indwelling help of the HOLY SPIRIT. So be obedient and get baptised like JESUS did and His disciples baptised others too.

Do you have occult objects or sinful worldly things in your home, pornographic content, violent movies, anything to do with witchcraft, harry potter etc? You can't hope to hear well from GOD if you're harbouring sin and demons in your home. Yes they can use that stuff as legal rights to enter your environment. You think this is all a joke? Nope. I've had a demon astral project into my home and seen them with my own two eyes in the flesh. The satanic is real.

James 1:7-8 AMP:

> **“For such a person ought not to think or expect that he will receive anything [at all] from the Lord, being a double-minded man, unstable and restless in all his ways [in everything he thinks, feels, or decides].”**

So, are you double minded? Decide today to put GOD first. Get rid of the worldly junk, attitudes, behaviors, mindsets and lifestyle that's not Godly. You know it's not right. There's that nagging sensation in your mind. You don't have peace about what you're doing or your life. That's because the time is short and you're on the fence. You're playing the world and trying to give GOD the scraps. You turn up on Sundays and do whatever you want the rest of the week. That's foolish virgin territory... left behind to suffer the tribulation.

> **“Enter through the narrow gate. For wide is the gate and broad and easy to travel is the path that leads the way to destruction and eternal loss, and there are many who enter through it. But small is the gate and narrow and difficult to travel is the path that leads the way to [everlasting] life, *and there are few who find it*.**

Revelation 3:10-11  Amplified Bible:
