Every preaching pray:
Dear LORD,
1. The power of YOUR Word, GOD, is powerful, please open hearts and minds to receive it and spread it to wherever it needs to go. May it find fertile soil in their hearts and may no-one steal the Word and may no weeds choke its growth. And may no moderators delete the post.
2. And LORD, please help me to hear YOU clearly without interference and have the courage to follow YOUR will and plans for my life. And please help me to love YOU with all my spirit, soul, body, heart and mind, seek GOD first in everything, YOUR righteousness and the Kingdom of GOD and love my neighbour as myself.
3. And LORD, please flow with me and make me flow with YOU to write this sermon.
4. And LORD, please make sure the enemy or their agents do not comment on this sermon or disrupt it by downvoting it or reporting it or deleting it.
5. And LORD, please bring about real change in the lives of those who read this sermon.
