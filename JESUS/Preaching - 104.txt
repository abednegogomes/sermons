Is this relevant for anyone?


Revelation 2:4-5 AMP:

> **But I have this [charge] against you, that you have left your first love [you have lost the depth of love that you first had for Me]. So remember the heights from which you have fallen, and repent [change your inner self—your old way of thinking, your sinful behavior—seek God’s will] and do the works you did at first [when you first knew Me]; otherwise, I will visit you and remove your lampstand (the church, its impact) from its place—unless you repent.**

It's a tough scripture, but it's spot on. Sometimes we lose sight of JESUS and GOD in the midst of everything else that's going on and we compromise, we lose sight of the love of our Saviour and start living in worldly ways.
So, we need to repent and do the good things we did when we first knew JESUS. What were those? Worship, praying daily, reading the Word daily, staying out of sin and worldly immoral behaviors, getting fed with the Word at your local church, being part of the community of other believers, helping others etc.

Please do a self analysis and see if that's what's going on in your life or if you feel the conviction of the HOLY SPIRIT. These verses tie in with the [parable of the 10 virgins](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%2025&version=AMP). Some are ready, some get left outside when JESUS returns.
