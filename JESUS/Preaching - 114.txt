A warning to the rich


James 5:3 Amplified Bible:

> **Your gold and silver are corroded, and their corrosion will be a witness against you and will consume your flesh like fire. You have stored up your treasure in the last days [when it will do you no good].**

Here we see that hoarding wealth will be of no use in the last times, especially the end times judgements. The super rich won't able to hide in bunkers in New Zealand or escape what's coming with blood infusions. In the end they must wrestle with their own mortality, something they can't hide from or escape, because only JESUS can give us eternal life.

John 10:7 Amplified Bible:

> **So Jesus said again, “I assure you and most solemnly say to you, I am the Door for the sheep [leading to life].**

So, why waste your time pursuing eternal life through wealth, technology and other nonsense? Your faith is what is lacking.
