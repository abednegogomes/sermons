Hearing GOD for yourself


The main reason when we can't hear GOD for ourselves is that we aren't even trying. Or there is too much distraction going on around us, or in our minds.

1. Get alone in a quiet room. By yourself. No noise. No distractions. Quiet instrumental worship music in the background is optional.
2. Quieten your mind to stop thinking various things by praying/declaring the following things out loud:
- "I submit myself to You GOD, spirit, soul, body, heart, mind, thoughts and emotions and I resist the devil, in the mighty name of my LORD JESUS CHRIST."
- "I rebuke, bind and firewall off all voices of the enemy from entering into my heart and mind, in the mighty name of my LORD JESUS CHRIST."
- "I repent of any lies I am believing and I renounce those lies and deceptions right now, in the mighty name of my LORD JESUS CHRIST."
- "I raise up Your voice GOD in my mind to 100% volume and I quiet down my own internal voice to 0% volume, in the mighty name of my LORD JESUS CHRIST."
- Now listen. Simple faith. Say hello. Ask a question, whatever. And wait for the answer. What do you hear back? That still, small voice in your mind. Yes, it's GOD.
- Write down what you hear, if you want to, I find this really helps. You can write something on paper like a question, then write down the response from GOD after. Later you will look back and see that the response was not yourself (or even your own style of writing).

Those with the HOLY SPIRIT in them, will hear His voice clear as day. No, you're not hallucinating or imagining it. Millions have heard His voice throughout time and history, even today. Sometimes it helps speaking/praying back out loud so your thoughts don't get mixed in with GOD's, or writing them down so there's a clear delineation. However know that GOD can hear all our thoughts, see Psalm 139:1-4, Matthew 9:4 and various other scriptures. 

If you're not saved and don't hear the HOLY SPIRIT for yourself, well, it's simple, go pray the sinner's prayer out loud, from the heart so you mean it. Get water baptised, then pray and ask to be baptised with the HOLY SPIRIT.

Bonus tips for *really* hearing GOD clearly, especially when you have a big decision to make, or are lacking direction:
- Fast from food for 1-3 days at least.
- Pray in tongues (your heavenly language) for extended periods e.g. 30 minutes.
- Read your own Bible daily. How many people don't do this?!
- Stop filling your mind with junk and filth from the internet, worldly TV shows, movies and music.

Know that you can pray/talk to individual Persons of the Trinity just by saying their name and directing the question/conversation to FATHER GOD, LORD JESUS, or HOLY SPIRIT. You can be sure each of them love you so much and want an individual relationship with you, and for you to grow in your personal relationship and walk with them. Each are different and have a different voice and personality. Think of FATHER GOD as your Heavenly DAD, the best DAD in the universe, LORD JESUS as your older brother and best friend, and HOLY SPIRIT as your trusted friend, teacher, advocate, comforter and counselor. 

John 16:13 Amplified Bible:

> **But when He, the Spirit of Truth, comes, He will guide you into all the truth [full and complete truth]. For He will not speak on His own initiative, but He will speak whatever He hears [from the Father—the message regarding the Son], and He will disclose to you what is to come [in the future].**

I invite anyone reading this to try it for themselves. Don't rely on your pastor at church or going to church every Sunday to tick the box to say you've done your bit. It's your responsibility to pray with them and have your own relationship with GOD.

Matthew 7:21-23 Amplified Bible:
