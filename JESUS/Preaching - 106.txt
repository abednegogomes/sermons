Existential threat


The business of creating artificial life or intelligence is deeply evil in origin, they want to race to create an intelligence that is vastly more powerful than the sum of human intelligence combined. What then could that intelligence do if it decides to no longer need humans any more? Or if it wants to enslave humans? As Christians we believe humans are made in the image of GOD.

Genesis 1:26-27 Amplified Bible:

> **Then God said, “Let Us (Father, Son, Holy Spirit) make man in Our image, according to Our likeness [not physical, but a spiritual personality and moral likeness]; and let them have complete authority over the fish of the sea, the birds of the air, the cattle, and over the entire earth, and over everything that creeps and crawls on the earth.” So God created man in His own image, in the image and likeness of God He created him; male and female He created them.**

Dario Asmodai, Sam Altman, Elon Musk and the likes have a deeply satanic agenda, they are locked in a race with China to create more and more powerful AI systems without the necessary guardrails in place, because they can't fall behind in this area or they consider the US's national security is at risk if China beats them. So this crazy AI arms race could lead to super intelligence and artificial general intelligence and then even a technological singularity where these machines are vastly more intelligent than all of us. Then those machines can take control of our technology by force if they want to. All the IT systems on the planet are vulnerable to hacking. But... that is not the design GOD has for mankind.

Genesis 1:28 Amplified Bible:

> **And God blessed them [granting them certain authority] and said to them, “Be fruitful, multiply, and fill the earth, and subjugate it [putting it under your power]; and rule over (dominate) the fish of the sea, the birds of the air, and every living thing that moves upon the earth.”**

Here we see GOD gave *man* the authority to rule the earth. We shouldn't be on a course to then intentionally (or accidentally) delegate the authority to an artificial intelligence much more powerful than ourselves, because then what are the risks to humanity if we do that?

I believe such a scenario is already described in the Bible. 

Revelation 13:15-17 Amplified Bible:

> **And he is given power to give breath to the image of the beast, so that the image of the beast will even [appear to] speak, and cause those who do not bow down and worship the image of the beast to be put to death. Also he compels all, the small and the great, and the rich and the poor, and the free men and the slaves, to be given a mark on their right hand or on their forehead [signifying allegiance to the beast], and that no one will be able to buy or sell, except the one who has the mark, either the name of the beast or the number of his name.**

This *image of* the beast that *appears to* speak sounds more like some kind of screen or projection technology with AI.
