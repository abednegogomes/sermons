The evil king over all that are proud


We find it here in [Job 41](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Job%2041&version=AMP).

Job 41:1, 34 Amplified Bible:

> “Can you draw out Leviathan with a fishhook? Or press down his tongue with a cord?

> “He looks on everything that is high [without terror]; **He is monarch over all the sons of pride.** [And now, Job, who are you who does not dare to disturb the beast, yet who dares resist Me, the beast’s creator? Everything under the heavens is Mine; therefore, who can have a claim against God?]”

In the Bible, when satan (the dragon/the devil/the serpent) fell and took 1/3rd of the angelic host to rebel with him [Revelation 12:3-9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation%2012%3A3-9&version=AMP) it is thought that leviathan aligned with them. From my readings of other sources it appears to be a primordial creature of chaos. In the literal hierarchy of fallen angels, leviathan is usually ranked even higher than a principality. It is identified as a former "Prince of the Seraphim" (the highest order of angels) who fell alongside lucifer and beelzebub. Now leviathan is named as one of the seven princes of hell.

The symptoms associated with this spiritual influence almost always revolve around pride, deep-seated miscommunication, and spiritual blindness.

***Severe Miscommunication and Misunderstanding:***

- Twisting Words: A person under this influence regularly misinterprets the innocent words of others as personal attacks. Conversely, they find that no matter how hard they try to explain themselves, their own words come out sounding harsh or wrong to others.

- The "Deaf and Dumb" Effect: When spiritual truths, correction, or emotional appeals are spoken to them, it is as if they literally cannot hear or process the information. The communication lines are completely blocked.

***Intellectual Pride and Unteachability:***

- Always Right: There is an absolute inability to admit fault, say "I'm sorry," or show vulnerability.

- Spiritual Superiority: They may look down on others' spiritual walk, believing they possess deeper insights, secret knowledge, or a higher level of righteousness.

- Defensiveness: Even the gentlest critique triggers an immediate, aggressive defense mechanism to protect the ego.

***Spiritual Blindness and Deception:***

- Inability to See Self: The person is entirely blind to their own flaws or the damage they are causing to relationships. They genuinely see themselves as the victim in every scenario.

- Distrust of Authority: They harbor a deep-seated suspicion toward spiritual leaders, mentors, or anyone in a position of authority, assuming they have ulterior motives.

***Sowing Discord and Division:*** Where leviathan goes, turbulent waters follow.

- Gossip and Accusation: The influence often manifests as subtle tongue-wagging, fault-finding, and spreading whispers that cause friends, families, or church communities to turn against one another.

- Breaking Covenants: Because pride prevents reconciliation, relationships under this influence tend to end abruptly and destructively rather than healing through forgiveness.

***The Path to Breaking the Influence***

In pastoral care, because leviathan is rooted entirely in pride, the only antidote is its exact opposite: humility and submission to GOD. Deliverance from this specific mindset rarely comes through verbal confrontation. Instead, it requires the individual to actively practice self-examination, intentionally lower themselves, fast and pray, confess and repent of hidden faults, and invite trusted, mature people to speak hard truths into their life.

James 4:6-7 Amplified Bible:

> But He gives us more and more grace [through the power of the Holy Spirit to defy sin and live an obedient life that reflects both our faith and our gratitude for our salvation]. Therefore, it says, **“GOD IS OPPOSED TO THE PROUD AND HAUGHTY, BUT [continually] GIVES [the gift of] GRACE TO THE HUMBLE [who turn away from self-righteousness].”**

> **So submit to [the authority of] God.** Resist the devil [stand firm against him] and he will flee from you.

***Submission Disarms the Scale of Pride:***

- The biblical leviathan is covered in tightly interlocking scales that keep the light out. Submission to GOD is an act of vulnerability, it peels those protective, defensive scales back and allows GOD's truth to enter.

- Alignment Creates the Authority: The enemy doesn't flee because a human tells him to; he flees because he recognizes the authority of the GOD that human is fully submitted to.

If someone is dealing with the confusion, twisting of words, and stubbornness that comes with a proud mindset, the battle isn't won by arguing or trying to prove oneself right. It’s won by dropping to one's knees, repenting of prideful attitudes and self-reliance, and submitting entirely to GOD. Once that alignment is fixed, the enemy simply loses his grip.
