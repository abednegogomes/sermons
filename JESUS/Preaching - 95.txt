Giving to those in need


Matthew 5:42 Amplified Bible:

> **Give to him who asks of you, and do not turn away from him who wants to borrow from you.**

This is a hard principle of JESUS's to follow, right? You may never see that money again. But, I do think it is good to give a helping hand where needed. I do think it is best to be wise in *what* you give them, e.g. is it an actual need they have, or do they turn around and spend the money on alcohol, cigarettes, drugs and their addictions behind your back.
