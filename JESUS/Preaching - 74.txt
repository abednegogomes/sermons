Leave vengeance for the LORD


Judges 1:6-7 Amplified Bible

> **But Adoni-bezek fled; and they pursued him and caught him and cut off his thumbs and his big toes. 7 Adoni-bezek said, “Seventy kings with their thumbs and big toes cut off used to gather up scraps of food under my table; as I have done [to others], so God has repaid me.” So they brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.**

Many times we think about getting even on our own, but we don't need to. Leave the issue in GOD's Hands with prayer.

Romans 12:19 Amplified Bible:

> **Beloved, never avenge yourselves, but leave the way open for God’s wrath [and His judicial righteousness]; for it is written [in Scripture], “VENGEANCE IS MINE, I WILL REPAY,” says the Lord.**

We can trust GOD to do what He says He will do.

Psalm 65:5:

> **By awesome and wondrous things You answer us in righteousness, O God of our salvation,**

> **You who are the trust *and* hope of all the ends of the earth and of the farthest sea;**
