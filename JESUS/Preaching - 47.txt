Striving to follow JESUS


The meaning of life is to follow JESUS.

Please get this into your way of daily living. Don't get distracted in your daily activities without asking Him to join you. He loves participating in life with you. Talk to Him while doing chores. Discuss your daily work and plan together. Involve Him.

You might ask, how do I talk with Him? Well just say things out loud (which helps solidify your thinking). Or think back in your head. He *can* read our thoughts. And those baptised believers with the HOLY SPIRIT *can* hear a voice replying back to them. No it's not your imagination. Test the spirits.
