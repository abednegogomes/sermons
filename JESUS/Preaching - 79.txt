For you shall know the truth and the truth shall set you free


First of all, let's clear some things up. In biblical scripture, a strict distinction is drawn between Moral Law (transcendent righteousness grounded in GOD's holy character) and Ceremonial / Civil Law (Israel's dietary rules, ritual purification rites, and civil temple administration). While ceremonial laws (e.g. Levitical dietary prohibitions or ceremonial feast days) were fulfilled and rendered non-binding in the New Covenant (Acts 10:9–15, Colossians 2:16), ***zero* moral sins are ever whitelisted or sanctioned in the New Testament**. Moral transgressions permanently remain *prohibited*.

Romans 3:23-24 Amplified Bible:

> **since all have sinned and continually fall short of the glory of God, and are being justified [declared free of the guilt of sin, made acceptable to God, and granted eternal life] as a gift by His [precious, undeserved] grace, through the redemption [the payment for our sin] which is [provided] in Christ Jesus,**

As Christians we don't condemn or judge others for their sins, because we can be guilty of them ourselves, either in the past or currently. In fact there are so many sins, it is impossible not to sin unless you have the abiding help of the HOLY SPIRIT living in you. It is the HOLY SPIRIT's job to convict people of their sin and bring them to repentance. However as Christians it _is_ in our purview to point out what GOD says in His Word is sin and what isn't. Because otherwise you will get conflicting information from the world and these poor people risk going about their lives thinking they're in right standing with GOD, but are actually deceived and in danger of going to hell.
