Apostasy


Here we see Paul riling people up:

Acts 23:7 AMP:

> **“When he said this, an angry dispute erupted between the Pharisees and the Sadducees, and the [whole crowded] assembly was divided [into two factions]. ”**

Almost any Bible verse will rile up someone somewhere. Think about that for a second. If you're a believer,  you've trained yourself to submit to GOD and at least have an open mind or heart to hearing the Word of GOD. For outsiders though, the words can be piercing. Why are they piercing? Because:

Hebrews 4:12-13 AMP:

> **“For the word of God is living and active and full of power [making it operative, energizing, and effective]. It is sharper than any two-edged sword, penetrating as far as the division of the soul and spirit [the completeness of a person], and of both joints and marrow [the deepest parts of our nature], exposing and judging the very thoughts and intentions of the heart. And not a creature exists that is concealed from His sight, but all things are open and exposed, and revealed to the eyes of Him with whom we have to give account.”**

And we're told that:

2 Timothy 3:16-17  Amplified Bible:

> **All Scripture is God-breathed [given by divine inspiration] and is profitable for instruction, for conviction [of sin], for correction [of error and restoration to obedience], for training in righteousness [learning to live in conformity to God’s will, both publicly and privately—behaving honorably with personal integrity and moral courage]; so that the man of God may be complete and proficient, outfitted and thoroughly equipped for every good work.**

So, if you really want to be a man or woman of GOD, get stuck into your Bible and let it and the HOLY SPIRIT convict you of sin, correct your errors, train you in righteousness so you're thoroughly equipped for every good work.

If any church is legitimate, they'll preach on the whole Bible, not tolerating any kinds of sexual sin, idolatry, blasphemy or anything else considered immoral. So don't be deceived by anyone, ***read the Word of GOD for yourself***. 

If you don't like it, tough biscuits. You're not GOD, you're not omnipotent, omnipresent or omniscient. Why should you, a mere peon brain have a say in the holy Word of GOD, ignoring the parts you don't like to suit your ideals? And that goes for any human on earth, from a baby to the pope. We're dust. Always default to the Word. It's been finalised since the early centuries. Any attempts to modernise it to be more permissive especially in the moral law is *doctrine of demons*.
