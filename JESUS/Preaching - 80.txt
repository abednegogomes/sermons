Prayer in times of disaster


As we read in [1 Samuel, chapter 30](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20Samuel%2030&version=AMP), the amelekites had made a raid on the south country and burned the town of ziklag with fire, capturing all the women and children to be used as slaves. But we see David turned to GOD in prayer for advice:

1 Samuel 30:8 Amplified Bible:

> David inquired of the Lord, saying, “Shall I pursue this band [of raiders]? Will I overtake them?” And He answered him, “Pursue, for you will certainly overtake them, and you will certainly rescue [the captives].” 

Later on we see David and his men struck them down in battle and only four hundred young men escaped. And David recovered all that the Amalekites had taken, and rescued his two wives. Nothing of theirs was missing whether small or great, sons or daughters, spoil or anything that had been taken; David recovered it all.

***So, when disaster strikes, GOD has our back when we call out to Him.***
