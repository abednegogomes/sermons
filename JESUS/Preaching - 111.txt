A proof of GOD


I think there is compelling evidence in the Bible that GOD exists and that evidence exists in something seemingly mundane, the dietary laws.

When we read Deuteronomy and Leviticus, we see the dietary laws that were given by GOD to Moses to deliver to the Israelites as part of the Mosaic Covenant which were written about 1450-1400 BC (estimate). These were primarily for the Israelites to maintain ritual purity, express obedience, and remain holy (or set-apart people) distinct from the surrounding pagan nations.

*But*, if we look closer at the animal and blood prohibitions and permissions, we can see a pattern emerge. Something in these laws is *far too wise* for the bronze age Israelites at the time to have invented on their own. In fact a lot of the other nations around them would just eat whatever they felt like, so the wisdom wasn't common knowledge or anything like that.

Firstly, a lot of the prohibitions list animals that carry a high risk of disease and illness if you handle or eat them. Now that we are in 2026, we can see that time has shown that a lot of the prohibited animals caused epidemics and pandemics. And with modern science we now *know* that they do have a high risk of disease transmission. So, how do you explain the laws existing thousands of years ago before modern science? And we look specifically at the verse about animals being clean if they chew the cud. For example, a cow's 4 stomach system is amazing in that it properly filters the food and you're much less likely to get ill from eating the cow meat than say pig meat.

Secondly, the laws interestingly prohibit eating a lot of animals that have high intelligence compared to the clean animals that the Israelites were permitted to eat. Like in a way that we should respect these creatures because they may have a neocortex or similar (e.g. capable of conscious thought and abstract reasoning), that they're highly sentient life and we shouldn't treat them as a food source. Just like it's taboo to think about eating your dog or cat. However the Israelites back then wouldn't have had the knowledge or science to know that some life was highly intelligent, like chimpanzees, dolphins, elephants and so-on.

This is just a superficial investigation and isn't comprehensive on of all the animals, but I'm convinced that the unclean animals listed in the Bible were not just unclean from a spiritual purity perspective, but also, as we now know from modern science, coincidentally unclean from a scientific and biological perspective (more risky for humans to prepare and eat). Which indicates that the primitive Israelites may have received such wisdom from a higher power i.e. GOD after all to help keep them healthy and safe as well. It's a *little too coincidental* to say that these very specific laws *accidentally* help the Israelites stay safer from food borne diseases. We can see that wisdom e.g. to not eat pork, or bat meat is not prevalent or widely known in countries to this day. For example, in various countries they still eat bat, so that excludes it being common knowledge among societies.
