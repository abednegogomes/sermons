The greatest act of mercy.


Romans 5:8 Amplified Bible:

> **But God clearly shows *and* proves His own love for us, by the fact that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us.**

Here we see that FATHER GOD does love us. He even sent His only Son JESUS to die for us, the perfect sacrifice, that we may not be condemned if we believe in Him.

Would you like to know JESUS? Just ask Him into your heart today.
