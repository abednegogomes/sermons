Rest you who are weary and heavy laden



Deuteronomy 5:15 Amplified Bible:

> **You shall remember [with thoughtful concern] that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and that the Lord your God brought you out of there with a mighty hand and by an outstretched arm; therefore the Lord your God has commanded you to observe the Sabbath day.**

In some cultures and some professions you don't even get a day of rest, they just work 7 days a week. Just as GOD rested on the [seventh day of creation](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%202%3A1-3&version=AMP), so should we rest for at least one day every week so that we get adequate rest and restoration, relaxation and recreation. Money isn't everything in life. Prioritise your own health and well-being too. Too often people prioritise status and living in wealthy areas or having flash cars or nice houses. But actually, you don't need all that to be happy. Some of the happiest people I know are poor, but they're rich and joyful in spirit because they have a relationship with their Creator.
