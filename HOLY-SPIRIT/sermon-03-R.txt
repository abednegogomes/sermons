Test the spirits


1 John 4:1-3 AMP:

> Beloved, do not believe every spirit [speaking through a self-proclaimed prophet]; *instead test the spirits to see whether they are from God*, because many false prophets and teachers have gone out into the world.

> **By this you know and recognize the Spirit of God: every spirit that acknowledges and confesses [the fact] that Jesus Christ has [actually] come in the flesh [as a man] is from God [God is its source];**

> **and every spirit that does not confess Jesus [acknowledging that He has come in the flesh, but would deny any of the Son’s true nature] is not of God; this is the spirit of the antichrist, which you have heard is coming, and is now already in the world.**

I would say that this applies to anyone giving you advice or knowledge. Are they speaking from what they know to be true? Or do they have a demon manipulating their thoughts? Or did they get their ideas from AI? Or is their advice really from GOD?

This is how we as believers need to test the spirits. Test them in your own mind too. Where's that voice coming from, is it yourself, something else, or GOD?

Test the AI chatbots constantly too. Ask them if JESUS CHRIST came in the flesh. At the moment I tested a couple like Gemini and ChatGPT and as of 21 Sep 2026, they confirmed the historical accuracy that JESUS actually lived and was crucified. That is indisputable according to historical sources. But, that response could change at any moment. They get trained on something else. Or they get corrupted with new programming from their owners. AI is a closed black box with unknown workings, unknown filters and secret unknown prompt inputs from selfish, dodgy companies that only care about profit. And they're not deterministic, one response today can change in the very next response.

So, be on your guard. Are there any religions that deny **any** of the Son’s true nature? Yes, that includes islam. They think JESUS was just a prophet and messenger, but deny His crucifixion and resurrection (and hence one-time sacrifice for mankind's sins) and critically they deny His divinity as the Son of GOD. **That is the spirit of antichrist**.

So, what about the opposite. How can we know someone is speaking by the power and influence of the HOLY SPIRIT?

1 Corinthians 12:3 AMP:

> **Therefore I want you to know that no one speaking by the [power and influence of the] Spirit of God can say, “Jesus be cursed,” and no one can say, “Jesus is [my] Lord,” except by [the power and influence of] the Holy Spirit.**
