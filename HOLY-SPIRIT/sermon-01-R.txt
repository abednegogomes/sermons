Staying in GOD's love


Deuteronomy 5:10 Amplified Bible, Classic Edition:

> **And showing mercy and steadfast love to thousands and to a thousand generations of those who love Me and keep My commandments.**

Jude 20-21 Amplified Bible, Classic Edition:

> **But you, beloved, build yourselves up [founded] on your most holy faith [make progress, rise like an edifice higher and higher], praying in the Holy Spirit;**

> **Guard and keep yourselves in the love of God; expect and patiently wait for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ (the Messiah)—[which will bring you] unto life eternal.**

Here it shows *how* we stay in GOD's love and mercy. Keep GOD's commandments and pray in the HOLY SPIRIT. For pentecostal and charismatic Christians that means praying in tongues, for those that don't have that gift, it means praying with the HOLY SPIRIT's guidance and direction, so listen for that still quiet voice that prompts you what to pray. Some need to practice listening for that quiet still voice. They are speaking, you're just not listening closely. You need to remove distractions and have some focus time with Them each day e.g. while reading your Bible or going somewhere quiet.
