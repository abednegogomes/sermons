Posted to: 

Good fruit


Please consider this verse.

[Mark 4:19 Amplified Bible](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark%204&version=AMP):

> **but the worries and cares of the world [the distractions of this age with its worldly pleasures], and the deceitfulness [and the false security or glamour] of wealth [or fame], and the passionate desires for all the other things creep in and choke out the word, and it becomes unfruitful.**

That's the majority of us in the world today. We hear the Word of GOD, but we get distracted with things of this world so we cannot produce good fruit in our lives.

Are you guilty of that? Repent. Read your Bible every morning when you can. Meditate (think) on the verses and ask HOLY SPIRIT to explain to you. Say the verse/s out loud so you hear them as well and can more easily commit them to memory. Apply the scriptures to your daily life and walk in communication (prayer) with HOLY SPIRIT as you go about your day.

Matthew 7:19 Amplified Bible:

> **Every tree that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.**

That's you if you live a spiritually fruitless life.
