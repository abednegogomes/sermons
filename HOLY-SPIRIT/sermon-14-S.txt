Let brotherly love continue

Psalms 133:1-3 AMP:

> **“Behold, how good and how pleasant it is For brothers to dwell together in unity! It is like the precious oil [of consecration] poured on the head, Coming down on the beard, Even the beard of Aaron, Coming down upon the edge of his [priestly] robes [consecrating the whole body]. It is like the dew of [Mount] Hermon Coming down on the hills of Zion; For there the Lord has commanded the blessing: life forevermore.”**

Is anyone at war with their own family, their own brothers? What's the point? Make peace.

I went to sit at the cemetery in the weekend and along came along a young couple. They knelt down and were fixing the flowers and rocks at a tombstone nearby with Killed in Action written on it. It was a young guy who had been killed in Ukraine a year earlier. I knew this because I looked up the name online. Sure enough it was his older brother visiting the grave as there was a photo of them together online. He died just 22 years old.

You never know when people will die. And all you'll have left of them is some flowers at a graveside. Make peace with your family.

Matthew 5:23-24 AMP:

> **“So if you are presenting your offering at the altar, and while there you remember that your brother has something [such as a grievance or legitimate complaint] against you, leave your offering there at the altar and go. First make peace with your brother, and then come and present your offering. ”**

And also more importantly:

Matthew 18:21-22 AMP:

> **“Then Peter came to Him and asked, “Lord, how many times will my brother sin against me and I forgive him and let it go? Up to seven times?” Jesus answered him, “I say to you, not up to seven times, but seventy times seven.”**

I didn't really know what to say to them. I didn't know if the guy was a Christian or not, he might be in hell or hades for all I know. Then they really would be gone. No hope of reunion in Heaven someday. That's the other sad thing about those who die believing in nothing. So stubborn that they're willing to die for all eternity and miss out on life with GOD and their eternal family in Heaven.

Matthew 7:13-14 AMP:

> **“Enter through the narrow gate. For wide is the gate and broad and easy to travel is the path that leads the way to destruction and eternal loss, and there are many who enter through it. But small is the gate and narrow and difficult to travel is the path that leads the way to [everlasting] life, and there are few who find it. [Deut 30:19; Jer 21:8]”**
