Get to know GOD

Deuteronomy 5:15 AMP:

> **“You shall remember [with thoughtful concern] that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and that the Lord your God brought you out of there with a mighty hand and by an outstretched arm; therefore the Lord your God has commanded you to observe the Sabbath day.”**

In the Old Testament commandments we have this one which is very important for a number of reasons. Don't fall into the trap of reading scripture at face value and moving on quickly like you would a book, that's the wrong way to read the Bible. Meditate (think) on each verse and ask the HOLY SPIRIT for wisdom, understanding and insight. And there may be more than one level of wisdom and interpretation baked in to each verse.

OK so what's the meaning behind this one.

1. At face level, the Israelites were once slaves, so GOD is telling them to rest one day a week so they don't perpetuate the slavery mindset, working themselves to death and living to work, like the egyptians forced them. And the preceeding verse commands them to let their own servants, animals and family rest too. So at face level, make sure you rest one day a week at least. This is Godly wisdom and we should apply it today too.

2. More on the *why*, we look at the preceding verse for context:

Deuteronomy 5:14 AMP:

> **“but the seventh day is a Sabbath [**a day of rest dedicated] to the Lord your God**; on that day you shall not do any work, you or your son or your daughter or your male servant or your female servant or your ox or your donkey or any of your livestock or the stranger who stays inside your [city] gates, so that your male servant and your female servant may rest as well as you. ”**

So here we see another real and deeper reason why. We're to dedicate the day to GOD. Rest and spend time with GOD. Talk and pray with them. GOD wants relationship with all of us individually. 

Christianity is not some stale religion of idol worship, repeating "hail Mary" and reciting scripted prayers. Actually it's a faith. And it's a relationship with the Living GOD. If you find yourself doing things by rote, you're doing it wrong. Learn to hear GOD's voiceGet to know GOD
 for yourself. If you're a HOLY SPIRIT filled Christian, that should come easily. Maybe that voice in your conscience _is_ GOD after all. Pray more. Get into GOD's presence.

If you're convicted of sin, repent and stop doing it. If you can't hear GOD, fast from food and pray for 3 days. You'll hear them. And you'll know what you need to fix in your life to get right with GOD again. You can't just live your life doing whatever and living like the world lives. Sin separates us from GOD. JESUS is crystal clear here:

John 15:5-6  Amplified Bible:

> **I am the Vine; you are the branches. The one who remains in Me and I in him bears much fruit, for [otherwise] apart from Me [that is, cut off from vital union with Me] you can do nothing. If anyone does not remain in Me, he is thrown out like a [broken off] branch, and withers and dies; and they gather such branches and throw them into the fire, and they are burned.**

If you're not in daily communion with GOD, you're withering. Soon about to die and be thrown out. That's got to be a wake up call for some of you.

Some of the deadest Christians are those that go to church every week to listen to sermons and have no daily personal relationship with their LORD and Saviour JESUS CHRIST, HOLY SPIRIT and FATHER GOD. Yes you can talk to them individually. And for those that are practiced in hearing them clearly, they have their own individual personalities and way of talking to you. So get to know them for yourself. 

Some of the even deader Christians are praying to Mary the earthly mother of JESUS or long dead saints. It's like Saul of the Old Testament getting a witch/medium to talk to the prophet Samuel who had died. See 1 Samuel 28 and his unfortunate consequences if you want to follow in his footsteps. Honestly, wouldn't you rather pray to the *Creator* rather than the created? This is unscriptural to pray to the dead. Pray to the living GOD. And you don't need a pope or priest as confessional or intercessor.
