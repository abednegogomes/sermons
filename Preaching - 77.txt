We must forgive


Matthew 6:14-15 Amplified Bible:

> **For if you forgive others their trespasses [their reckless and willful sins], your heavenly Father will also forgive you. But if you do not forgive others [nurturing your hurt and anger with the result that it interferes with your relationship with God], then your Father will not forgive your trespasses.**

1 John 3:15 Amplified Bible:

> **Everyone who hates (works against) his brother [in Christ] is [at heart] a murderer [by God’s standards]; and you know that no murderer has eternal life abiding in him.**

I think these verses are really, super important to remember. Because harbouring ill will, unforgiveness and hatred in your heart towards someone can be an open doorway for the demonic to use you to attack people. I have been on the receiving end of that with two different toxic relationships, in two separate incidents, where the women were holding onto hatred from their past relationship and life issues and they would manifest with a demonic spirit and threaten me with big 30cm (12 inch) knives from the kitchen and threaten to kill me. Yep, it was like a switch flipped and something else took over them and they lost control of themselves in their rage and hatred.

Matthew 18:21-22 Amplified Bible:

> Then Peter came to Him and asked, “Lord, how many times will my brother sin against me and I forgive him and let it go? Up to seven times?” Jesus answered him, “**I say to you, not up to seven times, but seventy times seven.**

So it's right there in black and white. We ***must*** forgive. And sometimes that is *hard*, especially with the horrible things that happen to us in life. But, the first step is saying out loud "*I forgive ______ (person's name) for ___________ (the specific thing they did or said) and I bless them, in the name of JESUS*". Then repeat that for all the different things that come to mind around what they did or said. Sometimes specifically forgiving for the individual offences is better than a blanket forgiveness. Especially if the issue keeps coming up in your mind at different times because it means you are dwelling on it and not releasing it.

Then you pray "*LORD, I release to You all this unforgiveness, bitterness, hurt, anger, rage, hatred and judgement to You, please give me your peace and justice in these situations.*" Then you leave it in GOD's Hands and leave GOD to deal with the person. Because:

Deuteronomy 32:35 Amplified Bible:

> **‘Vengeance is Mine, and retribution, In due time their foot will slip; For the day of their disaster is at hand, And their doom hurries to meet them.’**

But also remember this verse:

2 Peter 3:9 Amplified Bible:

> **The Lord does not delay [as though He were unable to act] and is not slow about His promise, as some count slowness, but is [extraordinarily] patient toward you, not wishing for any to perish but for *all* to come to repentance.**
