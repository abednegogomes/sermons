Combatting the lies, fear and doubt


Hebrews 4:12 Amplified Bible:

> **For the word of God is living and active and full of power [making it operative, energizing, and effective]. It is sharper than any two-edged sword, penetrating as far as the division of the soul and spirit [the completeness of a person], and of both joints and marrow [the deepest parts of our nature], exposing and judging the very thoughts and intentions of the heart.**

Ephesians 6:17 Amplified Bible:

> **And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the Word of God.**

Sometimes, like [JESUS in the wilderness](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%204%3A1-11&version=AMP), we may face temptation or circumstances that cause us to: doubt, fear or want to run and hide. But the HOLY SPIRIT will bring scriptures to our mind that we can use in the moment, so we can declare and decree with confidence the scripture out loud to combat the lie or thing affronting us at that time. Sometimes that may just be a lie or negative thought that pops into our head, but this is why it's important to know your Bible inside out so you don't believe the lie and go down a wrong path. Believe GOD's truth instead and that He loves us.
