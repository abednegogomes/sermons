Temptation, sin and how to overcome it


Please listen carefully to what I am about to tell you...

The reason we are in a battle is because of satan and his rebellion against Almighty GOD. satan is out to destroy GOD's creation and send as many people to hell as he can by tempting them into sin and also into their own rebellion against GOD.

Revelation 12:7-9 Amplified Bible:

> **And war broke out in heaven, Michael [the archangel] and his angels waging war with the dragon. The dragon and his angels fought, but they were not strong enough and did not prevail, and there was no longer a place found for them in heaven. And the great dragon was thrown down, the age-old serpent who is called the devil and satan, he who continually deceives and seduces the entire inhabited world; he was thrown down to the earth, and his angels were thrown down with him.**

Please consider this, if GOD is good then satan is the opposite of good, i.e. evil. All the evil in the world, can be traced back to satan and his lie in the garden of eden, where he questions GOD's character and makes Eve doubt GOD's truth, this is in [Genesis 3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%203&version=AMPC).

Since then, FATHER GOD loved us so much that He sent His Son JESUS as a sacrifice to die for our sins, so that we may find a way back to GOD. All we need to do is believe in JESUS and follow Him. That means giving up our sinful, worldly ways and trying our best (with the help of the HOLY SPIRIT) to live a righteous life.

Matthew 6:33 Amplified Bible:

> **But first and most importantly seek (aim at, strive after) His kingdom and His righteousness [His way of doing and being right—the attitude and character of God], and all these things will be given to you also.**

Now, that is easier said than done, there are constant temptations and worldly things that flood our minds daily. I find these 4 things most helpful to stay pure and free of fleshly desires.

1. Read your Bible. This is GOD's codebook between you and Him. If you know your Bible well and you're walking with the HOLY SPIRIT, you will hear verses coming in your head, quickly reminding you of what to do, or how to think in certain situations.

2. Pray in tongues. This strengthens your spirit more than your body (flesh), see [Romans 8:3-5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans%208%3A3-5&version=AMP). Speak to GOD in your heavenly language often, like 30 minutes per day. If you're lazy about this you will find that you will slip back into fleshly thinking and behaviour more frequently. If you don't have this gift, ask the HOLY SPIRIT for it.

3. Pray, pray, pray. Daily: [Matthew 6:9-13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%206%3A9-13&version=AMP) Pray the LORD's prayer i.e. "do not lead us into temptation, but deliver us from evil". [James 4:7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=james%204%3A7&version=AMP) Say out loud "I submit myself to GOD and resist the devil". [Philipians 4:13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Philippians%204%3A13&version=AMP) Declare out loud that "I can do all things through Him who strengthens and empowers me".

4. Put your spiritual armour of GOD on as it says in [Ephesians 6:10-18](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians%206%3A10-18&version=AMP).
