Super fast


In our day we can ask GOD for help with whatever we are working on, simply ask JESUS for His flow each morning. E.g. "LORD JESUS, please give me Your flow today, and make me flow with You as we go about the day together."

And see if it makes a difference. I find it makes a _world_ of difference. I can get stuff done much quicker in my job and ideas come quickly for fixing issues and so on. You'll be surprised about the timing of a lot of things. For me, my bus will arrive just at the moment I get to the stop. My train will be waiting on the platform ready to go in 1 minute of me getting on. Small things that add up to a smooth, enjoyable day without blockers and dramas.
